The purpose of this repository is to create an app that helps me learn to whistle by going over simplified sheet music of famous songs. The app shall act as a trainer: analyze the whistle through a selected audio source on the PC, isolate the dominant frequency, and based on the percieved tone and lenght of the note, only let the trainee proceed through the sheet music if they whistle the correct note.

This is a vibe coded app, AI will help me make the detailed specification, the user interface description, the way the program behaves, analyzes whistle notes and guides the user through the sheets. AI will do the implementation in a freely chosen programming language. The detailes of the implementation don't concern me, I am only interested in the end product behaviour, the UI.
