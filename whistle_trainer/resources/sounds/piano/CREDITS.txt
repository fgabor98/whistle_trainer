Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm

(Samples/<note>v10.flac).

Changes made for Whistle Trainer by scripts/make_piano_samples.py: 15 notes from C4 to F#7
at velocity layer 10, mixed to mono, trimmed to the hammer strike, retuned to exact
equal-tempered pitch, shortened to 4 seconds with a fade-out, normalized, and converted
to 16-bit WAV. Files are named by MIDI note number (60 = C4).
